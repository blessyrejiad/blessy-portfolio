About

I'm passionate about storytelling in all its forms: writing, journalism, film, music, and design. I believe that understanding human behaviour helps me create stories that are engaging, relatable, and easy to understand across different mediums.

I've been working across media, marketing, and communication since 2019, which means I've written about more topics than I can even remember — from technology and startup stories at The Economic Times to film scripts, social campaigns, and brand narratives.

I look at the world through a writer's lens. I see stories in everyday life just waiting to be told, and I do my best to be the medium through which they reach the page. Browse my portfolio and get in touch if you'd like to discuss working together.
